## Verdict definitions

- **Admitted:** the job produced a supported new conclusion and one or more
  new canonical evidence rows.
- **Admitted-bounded/no-new-row:** the job produced a supported positive or
  negative boundary, but existing canonical rows or a host-side reproduction
  already authenticated the necessary source material.
- **Duplicate/superseded:** another result resolved the same boundary more
  completely, so the job receives no separate useful-result credit.
- **Rejected:** the claimed conclusion did not survive source or semantic
  review.
- **Needs further research:** no durable conclusion could yet be drawn.

### Comparison with V7

V7 measured 21 admissions in 83m26s (15.1/hour end-to-end), a 33.4/hour busy
wave, and 2.430M campaign tokens/admission. On the present job-level useful
definition, the simplified batch measured 5.99/hour in its worker window and
8.068M generation+worker+curation tokens/useful result. Numerically, this first
batch is slower and about 3.32 times more token-intensive.

The figures are not like-for-like. V7 admissions were smaller canonical
packages produced within a different campaign/controller boundary; this batch
used broad research jobs, counts one useful verdict per job even when it
contains several relationships, and includes a separate human-triggered
curator pass. The defensible conclusion is therefore limited: the simplified
runner completed and preserved all 12 broad jobs without a retry or batch
failure, but this single two-slot wave does not demonstrate higher research
throughput or lower token cost than V7.
